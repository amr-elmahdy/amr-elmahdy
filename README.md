## Jurdian Project
<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/jurdian-banner.jpg" alt="Jurdian" width="100%">
</p>


A non-horror ARG built around ciphers, hidden information, and collaborative investigation. Inspired by Cicada 3301 — designed for the online world, with pieces that don't stay on the screen.

The first ARG of its kind in Egypt. Currently building the infrastructure: ciphers, tools, databases, platforms, and puzzles. New pieces get released over time, not all at once.
